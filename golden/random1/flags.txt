Under the GCDG 2025 multi-scaling regime (traffic-slackness δᵢ = rⁱ), the stationary distribution of the scaled SRBM (rZ₁, r²Z₂, …) converges to a product of independent exponentials with explicit means mₖ = uₖ⊤Γuₖ / (2 uₖ⊤R_{:,k}) (Theorem 1, eqs. 2.4–2.6).
