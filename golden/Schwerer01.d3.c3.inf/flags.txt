tractabilityExplanation:
All inter-arrival and service distributions are exponential and buffers are infinite. This document holds 3 customer classes, but no station is visited by more than one of them, so they are disjoint sub-networks rather than streams competing for a server: every station sees a single exponential class, and the document as a whole is the same stochastic object as one single-class Jackson network over all 3 stations. By Jackson's theorem the stationary joint distribution of station populations factorises; each station i behaves as an independent M/M/sᵢ queue with
    ρᵢ = αᵢ / (sᵢ · μᵢ)  < 1
    E[Nᵢ] = closed-form M/M/sᵢ mean (Erlang-C for sᵢ > 1, ρᵢ/(1−ρᵢ) for sᵢ = 1).
